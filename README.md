## Emotional Health and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

My article, **“Keeping Ourselves Emotionally Healthy Using Cognitive Behavioral Therapy,”** presents my personal study and understanding of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT). It covers emotions, negative thoughts, thought distortions, the Mood Shift Journal, relationship conflict resolution, and how the brain processes information during emotional distress.

**Read the article:** https://kumar-dileep.github.io/emotional_health.html
